# Ninja

Boss 2 in the Robot → Ninja → Cheese → Dave ladder. Defeat Robot to challenge this encounter. Victory unlocks the separate player configuration for both local players, saved in the existing browser progress.

Beat Ninja to recruit him and unlock Moonlit Dojo. Quick katana cuts trade reach and weight for speed. After a 0.62-second windup, Shadow form makes his body translucent at 35% opacity with no timeout. His marker and shadow stay visible. He can move and jump, and cannot be hit by attacks or hazards until he starts his next attack, even if that attack misses or is interrupted. Protection also clears on knockout or restart; it does not prevent falling off the page. Both the player and boss use this behavior.

The boss has 950 HP, attack armor, passive ultra charge, and enrage. The player has normal size, three lives, interruptible attacks, and combat-earned charge. Both configurations use `ultra.chargeMultiplier: 0.5`, halving charge from dealing damage, receiving damage, and passive boss charging (including enrage).

No Ninja reference image was present when this pack was created; the masked katana doodle uses the shared vector rig.
