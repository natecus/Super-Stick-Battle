# Ninja

Boss 2 in the Robot → Ninja → Cheese → Dave ladder. Defeat Robot to challenge this encounter. Victory unlocks the separate player configuration for both local players, saved in the existing browser progress.

Beat Ninja to recruit him and unlock Moonlit Dojo. Quick katana cuts trade reach and weight for speed. Shadow form replaces his old spinning ultra: after a 0.62-second windup his body becomes translucent at 35% opacity for five seconds (4.5 for the boss). His marker and shadow stay visible. He cannot be hit by attacks or hazards during the effect, and can still move and attack. The protection ends when its timer expires or he loses a life; it does not prevent falling off the page.

The boss has 950 HP, attack armor, passive ultra charge, and enrage. The player has normal size, three lives, interruptible attacks, and combat-earned charge.

No Ninja reference image was present when this pack was created; the masked katana doodle uses the shared vector rig.
