# Boomeranger

Based on `reference.jpeg`: a smiling stick figure with a boomerang in each hand.

Normal throws travel outward, turn back, and home toward their owner. Each opponent can be hit once on the outward trip and once on the return. Ka-boomerang is a larger ultra that explodes on enemy contact or when its fuse expires. Its blast does not hit its owner or teammates.

Edit `character.json` for stats and appearance. See [the character guide](../../../docs/ADDING-CHARACTERS.md).
